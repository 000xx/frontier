# Notice for this dataset

`estimates.csv` holds WHO's global estimates of malaria cases and deaths, 2000 to 2024, as published in the WHO Global Health Observatory and in the World malaria report 2025. Copyright in the data stays with the World Health Organization.

## What this means for the site

- Non-commercial, public-health use with credit is allowed under both sets of terms.
- If the site carries advertising, sells anything, or is run by a commercial entity, ask WHO for permission first (https://www.who.int/about/policies/publishing/copyright links the permissions form). The non-commercial condition of the report licence and the "no commercial promotion" condition of the data terms both bite.
- Do not redraw WHO's own charts or maps from the report; draw our own charts from the numbers.
- Show no WHO logo, and nothing that suggests WHO endorses the site.

The data terms ask for WHO, the dataset title, the year, the date of access, and acknowledgement of the countries that supplied the underlying data. Charts drawn from this file should carry:

> Source: WHO, Global Health Observatory: Estimated number of malaria cases and Estimated number of malaria deaths (World malaria report 2025), 2025, accessed 9 November 2026. Estimates are based on data reported by national malaria programmes of malaria-endemic countries. WHO does not endorse this site.
