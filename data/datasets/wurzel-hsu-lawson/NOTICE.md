# Notice for this dataset

`results.csv` is derived from the data set in the GitHub repository swurzel/lawson-criterion-paper (https://github.com/swurzel/lawson-criterion-paper), by Samuel E. Wurzel and Scott C. Hsu. It holds the experimental results compiled for:

- S. E. Wurzel and S. C. Hsu, "Continuing progress toward fusion energy breakeven and gain as measured against the Lawson criteria", Physics of Plasmas 32, 112106 (2025), https://doi.org/10.1063/5.0297357
- S. E. Wurzel and S. C. Hsu, "Progress toward fusion energy breakeven and gain as measured against the Lawson criterion", Physics of Plasmas 29, 062103 (2022), https://doi.org/10.1063/5.0083990

Credit: charts drawn from this data should carry the line "Data: S. E. Wurzel and S. C. Hsu, Phys. Plasmas 32, 112106 (2025)". The repository's README credits Bosch & Hale (1992) and Nevins & Swain (2000) for the fusion cross sections. Those were not used here.

The papers themselves are not copied: arXiv:2505.03834 is posted under the arXiv.org perpetual, non-exclusive licence, which grants no reuse of its text or figures, and the 2022 paper's arXiv licence was not confirmed. Only numerical results are used here.
